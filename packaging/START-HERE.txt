STFC ADVISOR - WINDOWS 11

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder you will keep, such as Documents\STFC Advisor.
2. Open the extracted STFC-Advisor folder and double-click STFC-Advisor.exe.
3. Your default browser opens the app. No Python installation is needed.
4. Optional: double-click Create-Desktop-Shortcut.vbs to add a desktop shortcut.

The app stays running in the Windows notification area (near the clock; it may
be inside the hidden-icons arrow). Right-click its gold/cyan icon for Open app,
Open data and logs, or Quit. Closing the browser tab leaves syncing running.
Double-clicking the EXE again opens the existing app.

FIRST SETUP
The package starts with a blank account. In Account & research, paste your shared
STFC Officers Tool Google Sheet link and choose Enable auto-sync, or restore an
account JSON you have intentionally exported. Add your owned ships in My fleet;
the officer sheet does not contain a complete owned-ship inventory.

SYNC
Google Sheet syncing checks every five minutes while the app runs and your PC
is awake and online. The connection is remembered across launches. Quit stops
syncing. This app does not add itself to Windows startup.

DATA AND UPDATES
Your account, sync settings, backups and app.log are stored separately at:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\STFC Advisor\profiles
Use Open data and logs in the tray menu to find them.
Before replacing the app folder with an updated version, choose Quit. Your
saved account remains in the data folder. Recreate the shortcut if you move
the app folder. Do not copy app.log or your account to a public issue report.

IF IT DOES NOT OPEN
Check the tray first. Read app.log in the data folder, or share the error text
with the person who supplied the app. This first build is not code-signed;
Windows may ask you to verify its publisher. Do not disable Windows security.

This is an estimated tactical advisor, not a complete STFC combat simulator.
